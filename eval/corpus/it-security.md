# Northwind Freight IT Security Policy

Sample data for the retrieval evaluation. Northwind Freight is a fictional company.

## Accounts and passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Password managers are provided to every employee and are the recommended way to store credentials.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the finance portal and all production systems. Hardware security keys are issued to administrators.

Accounts are disabled automatically after 45 days without a login.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock the screen after five minutes of inactivity.

Personal phones may access company email only through the managed mail app, which can be wiped remotely without touching personal data.

USB storage devices are blocked on all company laptops. Files are shared through the company drive instead.

## Data handling

Customer data is classified as confidential. It must never be pasted into public AI tools or personal cloud storage.

Production database backups are encrypted and kept for 35 days. Backups are restored as a test once every quarter.

## Incident reporting

Suspected phishing emails must be reported with the "Report phishing" button in the mail app, not forwarded.

Any security incident must be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery, by phone at extension 4444 or through the incident form.

## Access reviews

Managers review the system access of their team every six months. Access that is no longer needed is removed within five working days of the review.
