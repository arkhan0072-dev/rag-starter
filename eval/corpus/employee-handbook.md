# Northwind Freight Employee Handbook

Northwind Freight is a fictional logistics company. This handbook is sample data used to evaluate the retrieval pipeline.

## Working hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, Sunday to Thursday, from 8:30 to 17:30 with a one-hour lunch break.

Warehouse staff work in three rotating shifts. Shift rotas are published every second Wednesday so that staff have at least ten days of notice.

Employees may work from home up to two days per week if their role does not require them on site. Remote days must be agreed with the line manager in the team calendar.

## Leave

### Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, so a new joiner earns just over two days for each completed month.

Up to five unused days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March if they are not used.

### Sick leave

Employees must inform their manager before 9:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than two consecutive days.

Paid sick leave is capped at 15 days per year. Further absence is handled case by case with HR.

### Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive four weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time in the first year.

## Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase, with a photo of the receipt attached.

Claims above 500 USD need written approval from a department head before the purchase is made.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 45 USD per day. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Equipment

Engineers and office staff receive a laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every three years, or earlier if the device fails and cannot be repaired.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours so that the device can be locked remotely.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review covers goals, feedback from peers and a development plan.

Salary adjustments are decided once a year after the December review and take effect from 1 February.
