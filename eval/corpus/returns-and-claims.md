# Northwind Freight Returns and Claims

Sample data for the retrieval evaluation. Northwind Freight is a fictional company.

## Returns

Customers can book a return label from their account for up to 60 days after delivery. The return is collected from the original delivery address.

Return labels for standard parcels are free for business accounts on the Growth plan and cost 6 USD on the Starter plan.

## Damaged goods

Visible damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, together with photos of the parcel and its packaging. Hidden damage discovered after unpacking can be reported within seven days.

Keep the original packaging until the claim is closed, because an inspector may ask to see it.

## Lost parcels

A parcel is treated as lost when there has been no tracking event for ten working days. The customer can then open a lost-parcel claim in the portal.

## Compensation

Standard liability covers up to 100 USD per parcel without any extra charge.

Customers can buy extended cover for parcels worth up to 5,000 USD. Extended cover costs 1.5 percent of the declared value, with a minimum charge of 3 USD.

Approved claims are paid back to the original payment method within 14 days of approval.

## Disputes

If a customer disagrees with a claim decision, they can request a review within 30 days. The review is done by a team that was not involved in the first decision, and the outcome is final.
