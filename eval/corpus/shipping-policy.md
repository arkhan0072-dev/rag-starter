# Northwind Freight Shipping Policy

Sample data for the retrieval evaluation. Northwind Freight is a fictional company.

## Service levels

### Express

Express shipments are delivered the next working day within the same country when the parcel is collected before 14:00. Parcels collected after 14:00 are delivered within two working days.

### Standard

Standard shipments are delivered within three to five working days. Standard is the default service when a customer does not choose one.

### Economy freight

Economy freight is intended for pallets and bulk goods. Delivery takes seven to ten working days and includes tail-lift delivery at no extra cost.

## Weight and size limits

A single parcel may weigh up to 30 kg. Heavier items must be shipped as freight on a pallet.

The maximum parcel length is 120 cm, and the combined length and girth must not exceed 300 cm.

Pallets are limited to 1,000 kg and a height of 1.8 metres including the pallet itself.

## Restricted items

Lithium batteries can only be shipped when installed in the device they power. Loose lithium batteries are refused at collection.

Perfume, aerosols and other flammable goods require a dangerous-goods declaration and can only travel by road, never by air.

Cash, precious metals and live animals are not accepted under any service.

## Tracking

Every shipment receives a tracking number when the label is created. Tracking events are updated at least every four hours while the parcel is moving.

Customers can choose to receive tracking updates by SMS or email. SMS updates cost 0.10 USD per message for business accounts.

## Delivery attempts

The driver makes two delivery attempts on consecutive working days. After the second failed attempt, the parcel is held at the nearest depot for seven days and then returned to the sender.
